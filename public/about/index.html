About

The Hook

Hello, I’m r1skers.

I am an undergraduate Electrical Engineering student at <Yamagata University> in Japan.

Welcome to Hephaestus’s Forge. This site is my public “digital garden” and research notebook as I forge a path connecting the worlds of hardware (Verilog/FPGA), software (C/Python), and systems architecture.

The “Why”

I built this forge based on a simple realization:

As I study, new ideas and “sparks” often pop up. I found myself wanting a place to record these thoughts, allowing me to revisit, connect, and build upon them in the future.

This site is that record.

This process of “recording”—of finding something interesting, asking “why,” and summarizing my thoughts—is what I am most passionate about.

My long-term goal is to pursue a Ph.D. and become a “field-definer” who contributes new, original knowledge. This “forge” is the public record of that journey, starting with these first sparks.

My Philosophy

My approach to this “marathon” is built on two principles:

  1. High Focus over Haste I believe in strategic, deep learning, not short-sighted gains. This blog is where I practice that focus by “summarizing” my work and “learning by teaching”. My foundational posts are categorized under <Promethean Fire>, which will support my future <Artifact>.

  2. Energy Management This forge is also my “active rest”. I use the process of writing—whether it’s <Delphic Musings> or technical logs—to manage my energy, process my thoughts, and sustain this long-term campaign.

My Path Forward

I am currently focused on preparing for a top-tier graduate program, with the ultimate goal of pursuing a Ph.D..

My ambition is to “carve out a new field of my own” , applying the “find-experiment-summarize” process to areas that truly push the boundaries of technology.

My preparation for this journey involves:

  1. Building a deep, foundational understanding of complex subjects, which I document in <Promethean Fire`>.
  2. Applying that knowledge by building challenging “Artifacts” <Artifact>.
  3. Actively seeking challenging environments—be it a world-class graduate laboratory or a global internship—that will push me to the edge of my knowledge and force me to grow.

You can follow my “forging” process on my GitHub, or contact me directly.
